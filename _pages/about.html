About Me

Hi! I’m Shuning (Suzanna) Li, a senior undergraduate in the Tsien Excellence in Engineering Program at Tsinghua University.

I am currently a visiting student at UC Berkeley with Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka, working closely with Prof. Mingyu Ding. Previously, I interned at Sharpa.

My research interests include:

  • Humanoid robotics: loco-manipulation and whole-body control
  • Dexterous manipulation

I am seeking PhD opportunities in robotics. Please feel free to get in touch!
