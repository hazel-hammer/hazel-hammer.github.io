About Me

Hi! I’m Shuning Li 李姝凝, and I also go by Suzanna or Sue. I’m a senior undergraduate in the Tsien Excellence in Engineering Program at Tsinghua University, majoring in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics.

I’m currently a visiting student at UC Berkeley, working with Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka and Prof. Mingyu Ding. I’ve also had the opportunity to work under the guidance of Prof. Jiachen Li. Along the way, I was fortunate to work closely with Yuman Gao and Sikai Li.

My primary research interest is to connect embodied reasoning with reusable physical skills.

  • Robot Control: Humanoid loco-manipulation, whole-body coordination, and dexterous manipulation.
  • Hierarchical Systems: Bridging vision-language-action models and world action models with low-level physical skills.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027. Please feel free to get in touch!
